Each lab is built for one course and used inside its lessons, cut down to the step being taught. Here you get the whole instrument. The businesses, data and markets in them are fictional, made up so that every mistake a lab shows you is one it was designed to contain.

  • Accounting LabAnalyse transactions, post journal entries to T-accounts, and watch the trial balance and statements update.F01 Introduction to Financial Accounting
  • Data Cleaning LabInspect a messy dataset, choose cleaning steps, and see what each one does to the data and the conclusions.B02 Data Cleaning and Preparation
  • Marketing Mix SimulatorChoose a product, price, channels and promotion for a fictional launch and see simulated demand, margin and share.M02 The 4Ps Marketing Mix
