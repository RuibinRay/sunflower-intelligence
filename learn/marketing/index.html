Courses are listed in the order to take them, from introductory to advanced; each lists what it requires. Suggested order

If you are starting out, take the courses in this order: each comes after everything it requires, and the general before the specialised. Step 2, M02, is open now and starts from scratch; the steps before it are planned, and it teaches what it needs from them.
