      The 4Ps Marketing Mix

      Design a product, set its price, choose where it is sold and decide how it is promoted, and make the four decisions fit together.

      Start with lesson 1

      What you will learn

      • Explain the marketing mix and how each element creates or captures customer value.
      • Make product decisions across the product levels, product line and life cycle.
      • Set prices with cost-based, value-based and competition-based methods, and calculate break-even volume.
      • Use price elasticity to predict how revenue and profit respond to a price change.
      • Choose distribution channels and intensity, and build a price from factory to shelf.
      • Plan an integrated promotion mix, set its budget, and measure it with the right metrics.
      • Combine the four decisions into a consistent plan and defend it with evidence.

      Prerequisites

      • M01 Marketing Fundamentals Planned — recommended. Until it is written, this course teaches what it needs from it.

      How it works

      Each lesson teaches the idea from first principles, works examples step by step, lets you explore it in an interactive, then has you practise with feedback. A lesson is complete when you have worked its guided problems and scored 80% on its knowledge check. Mastery is tracked separately and comes back for review before you are likely to forget. Each module ends with a test; the course ends with a project and a final assessment.

      Fictional case

      Lantern Coffee Co.. A fictional coffee business founded by Ana Lim. It starts as a catering and barista-training service, opens an online shop for beans, then launches a canned cold brew. Every name and figure in it is invented for teaching.
