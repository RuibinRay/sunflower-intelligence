F08 · Applied project

Lantern’s hedging policy

It is October 2028. Hollis Avery, who owns 30% of Lantern, has asked Ana for a written hedging policy before Lantern signs its 2029 contracts. He wants to know what Lantern is exposed to, how each exposure will be handled and what risk will be left. Since booking the forward for the espresso machines, Ana has also ordered two grinders from Officine Varese (fictional), €24,000 due on 30 June 2029, so Lantern now owes euros on two dates.

You are working as Ana’s analyst. Measure the exposures, price the hedges on offer, design the coffee hedge, test it in a bad year for coffee, and write the policy. All figures are fictional; the method is the one a treasurer would use.

What you will produce

  • The size of Lantern’s euro and coffee exposures, and 2029 debt-service cover if coffee rose to $54 a kg with no hedge.
  • A fair price for a nine-month euro forward for the grinders, and the dollar cost at Coastline’s quote.
  • Lantern’s 2029 coffee bill under a policy of fixing 75% of its coffee and capping the rest.
  • 2029 EBITDA and debt-service cover under that policy in the $54 stress.
  • A one-page hedging policy for Hollis.
