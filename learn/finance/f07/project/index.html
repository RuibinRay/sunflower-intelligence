F07 · Applied project

Lantern’s five-year model for Coastline Bank

It is February 2028. Coastline Bank’s credit committee has read Ana’s model and her proposal: an $80,000 term loan instead of $60,000, with a stepped current-ratio covenant (at least 0.9 at the end of 2028, 1.0 in 2029 and 1.2 from 2030). Before it decides, the committee wants the model run on its own, more cautious assumptions, the committee case: online sales growing at 65% of Ana’s planned rates, café sales growing 3% a year, and 10% fewer cans of Cold Brew than planned. Everything else stays as in Ana’s base case.

You are working as Ana’s modeller. Rebuild the key parts of the model, run the committee case through it, and answer the committee with a recommendation it can act on. Every figure is fictional; the method is the one a real bank would use.

What you will produce

  • A check that the history is right, and the twelve-month run rate of 2027 EBITDA.
  • The committee case’s revenue and EBITDA, from the forecast worksheet.
  • The committee case’s current ratio at each year end, with the $60,000 and the $80,000 loan, and the smallest loan that would meet a flat 1.2 covenant.
  • The committee case’s equity value, compared with the base case.
  • A one-page memo to the credit committee with your recommendation.
