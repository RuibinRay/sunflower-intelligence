The full plan, school by school, with each course’s level, prerequisites and status. Courses are written one at a time in prerequisite order, and a course is marked complete only when every lesson, test and project is written, independently reviewed for accuracy and tested.

What the statuses mean

Complete
Every lesson, module test, the project and the final are written, independently reviewed for accuracy, tested (numbers, answer keys, interactions) and live.
Reviewed
Written and reviewed for accuracy; final testing still in progress.
In progress
Being written or checked. Lessons may be readable but are not yet complete.
Planned
An outline only. Nothing is written yet; the syllabus shows what it will cover.
