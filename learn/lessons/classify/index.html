A churn model gives each customer a probability. Turning that into a decision means choosing a threshold, and every threshold trades missed churners against false alarms.

The idea

Logistic regression keeps predictions between 0 and 1 by modelling the log-odds as a straight line. Raise e to a coefficient and you get an odds ratio: the factor by which one more unit multiplies the odds of the outcome, holding the other predictors fixed. Odds ratios multiply odds, not probabilities.

To act, choose a threshold and flag everyone above it. The confusion matrix counts the four results. Precision is the share of flagged customers who really churn; recall is the share of churners you flag. Lower the threshold and recall rises while precision falls. The ROC curve traces every threshold, and its area (AUC) is the chance a random churner scores above a random stayer: 0.5 is a coin flip, 1.0 perfect.

Clustering has no outcome column. k-means groups rows by distance, so put the columns on a common scale first (standardise each to mean 0, standard deviation 1), or whichever column has the bigger numbers decides the groups.

The formula

log p1 − p = b0 + b1x, p = 11 + e−(b0 + b1x), odds ratio = eb1, precision = TPTP + FP, recall = TPTP + FN

p
the predicted probability of the outcome (here, churn)
p ÷ (1 − p)
the odds: 0.25 probability is odds of 1 to 3
eb1
the odds ratio for one more unit of x
TP, FP, FN, TN

The common mistake

Reading an odds ratio as a ratio of probabilities

An odds ratio of 1.5 does not make churn 50% more likely. Two calls multiply the odds by 1.5² = 2.25, but the probability goes from 11.9% to 23.3%, only 1.96 times as high. The gap widens as probabilities grow: near certainty, the odds can keep multiplying while the probability barely moves.

Remember

  • ecoefficient is an odds ratio: it multiplies the odds per unit, holding the other predictors fixed.
  • Lowering the threshold raises recall and lowers precision. AUC summarises every threshold: 0.5 is chance.
  • Standardise columns before k-means, or the one with the biggest numbers dominates the distances.
