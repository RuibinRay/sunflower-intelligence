Start with the rate investors require, then value the business on the cash it should generate. The rate from the first tool can be sent straight into the second.

Cost of capital

The return investors expect for the risk they carry. Shareholders’ cost comes from the capital asset pricing model; lenders’ cost is cheaper after tax because interest is deductible. Weighted by market values, the two give the discount rate the DCF below uses.
