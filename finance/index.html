An obsession with yellow

In Arles, Van Gogh chased a yellow so intense he had to steel himself to reach it. This room chases the business equivalent: the cash a company really produces, and how safely it can keep producing it.

Five tools, in the order a valuation runs: the cost of capital, the value of a business from its cash flows, the price and interest-rate risk of a bond, the value of an option, and a screen for financial distress. Results update as you type, and every assumption stays on the page.

Cost of capital

The return investors expect for the risk they carry. Shareholders’ cost comes from the capital asset pricing model; lenders’ cost is cheaper after tax because interest is deductible. Weighted by market values, the two give the discount rate the DCF below uses.
