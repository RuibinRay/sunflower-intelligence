Company Valuation Studio

From a company’s financial statements to a defensible, reproducible valuation report: choose the models that fit, set every assumption, weigh the risks, and let the studio explain the result. Every calculation is shown; every figure is labelled as data, assumption or estimate.

The Valuation page builds a cost of capital and a one-line DCF. This studio takes the whole company: three to ten years of statements (typed in, uploaded, or mapped from SEC EDGAR), the model selection guide, forecasts driven by editable assumptions, comparables, dividend, asset-based and other models, scenarios with a risk register, and a report that explains its own numbers. It opens on a fictional company so the whole workflow can be tried before any real data is entered.

Everything runs in this browser tab. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere; the Save button downloads a JSON file you can reload later, and the page also keeps a copy in this browser’s local storage, which is convenient but neither secure nor shared across devices.

Workspace

Left: the company and its statements. Centre: the models, assumptions and results. Right: what it all means, the risks, and the report button. Guided mode walks through six steps and hides the advanced controls; advanced mode shows everything at once.

Company valuation report

Generated from the workspace above, so its numbers are the workspace’s numbers. Twelve sections, from the cover page to the technical appendix, each sentence produced by a rule tied to a calculated figure. Print it to PDF, download the tables, the model as an Excel workbook with live formulas, or the project as JSON.
