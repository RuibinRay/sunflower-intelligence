Marketing

Personal identity

Van Gogh painted the sunflower so often that it became his signature, the way a brand comes to own a colour. Owning a place in people’s minds starts with knowing them: who buys, how often, and what they will pay.

“You know that Jeannin has the peony, Quost has the hollyhock, but I have the sunflower, in a way.”Vincent van Gogh to Theo, Arles, 22 January 1889

Young sunflowers turn through the day to follow the sun; once mature, they settle facing east, where the morning warms them first and bees visit most. Customers and markets move too. These pages find the segments worth serving, the countries worth entering, the price worth charging and the growth worth paying for.
