The golden spiral

Look into a sunflower and its seeds line up in spirals, usually a Fibonacci number of them. Usually: in a citizen-science study published in 2016, volunteers counted the spirals on 657 sunflowers, and roughly a quarter of the counts broke the rule. Analytics is finding the pattern, then counting the exceptions honestly.

Swinton, Ochu & MSI Turing’s Sunflower Consortium, Royal Society Open Science, 2016

        Correlate

        How strongly do two columns move together? Pick the columns, then choose a cell to see the pair up close.

        Method

        Pearson measures straight-line relationships; Spearman ranks the values first, so it catches any steady rise or fall and resists outliers.

        Empty cells

        Pairwise keeps every row that has both values; listwise keeps only rows complete in every chosen column.

        Columns

        Correlation is not causation. Two columns can move together because one drives the other, because a third factor drives both, or by chance. Treat a strong correlation as a question to investigate, not an answer.

        Regress

        Ordinary least squares: how much does the outcome change with each predictor, holding the others fixed, and can the model be trusted?

            Segment

            k-means clustering finds groups of rows that sit close together across the columns you choose. Columns are put on a common scale first, and the silhouette score says how cleanly the groups separate.

            Columns to group on
            —silhouette score (−1 to 1; above 0.5 is a clear structure)

            • k-means always returns the number of groups you ask for, structure or not; a low silhouette says the groups are a convenient cut, not a discovery.
            • It favours round, similar-sized groups and is pulled by outliers. Results are seeded so they repeat.

            Forecast

            Holt-Winters exponential smoothing carries a series’ level, trend and seasonal pattern forward, with a range for how far off the forecast could be. Before trusting it, the tool hides the last season, forecasts it, and compares the result with simply repeating last year.

            periods

            12 for months in a year, 4 for quarters, 52 for weeks, 7 for days in a week.
