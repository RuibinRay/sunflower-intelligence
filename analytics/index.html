The golden spiral

Look into a sunflower and its seeds line up in spirals, usually a Fibonacci number of them. Usually: in a citizen-science study published in 2016, volunteers counted the spirals on 657 sunflowers, and roughly a quarter of the counts broke the rule. Analytics is finding the pattern, then counting the exceptions honestly.

Swinton, Ochu & MSI Turing’s Sunflower Consortium, Royal Society Open Science, 2016
